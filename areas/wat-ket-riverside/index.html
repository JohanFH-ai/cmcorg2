Cycling in Wat Ket and along the Ping

Across the river from the Night Bazaar, the old Wat Ket neighbourhood has three bike shops and direct access to the flat riverside roads.

  • 3 bike shops
  • 1 classic ride
  • Mueang Chiang Mai

Riding in Wat Ket

Wat Ket is the east bank of the Ping, a mix of old teak houses, riverside cafés and quiet lanes. Charoen Rat Road follows the river and is the natural start for a Ping River ride in either direction.

For a small area it is well served: Triple Cats Cycle specialises in touring bikes, the ORBEA store sells the Spanish brand, and Lek Bike on Kong Sai Road handles everyday repairs.

Getting there

Ride east from Tha Phae Gate and cross the Nawarat Bridge, about 1.5 km.

Landmarks to steer by

Nawarat Bridge · Charoen Rat Road · Wat Ket Karam · Iron Bridge · Ping River

Cycling in Wat Ket: questions riders ask

Where can I get a touring bike serviced in Chiang Mai?

Triple Cats Cycle on Bamrungrat Road in Wat Ket is the touring specialist in our directory.

What is the easiest ride from Wat Ket?

Follow the Ping River north or south. It is flat, and you can turn round whenever you like.
