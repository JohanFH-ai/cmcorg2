Cycling in Huay Kaew and Chang Phueak

North of the moat, between Chang Phueak Gate and Chiang Mai University. This part of town has more serious bike shops per kilometre than anywhere else in the city.

  • 4 bike shops
  • 3 classic rides
  • Mueang Chiang Mai

Riding in Huay Kaew and Chang Phueak

Huay Kaew Road runs from the north-west corner of the moat out to the zoo and the Doi Suthep road. Along it you will find Velocity and Arm Bikes, and a few blocks east on Thevarit Road is TCA, distributor for a long list of mountain bike brands.

The area is also the starting point for the flat ride north. Pick up the Canal Road near the university and follow it past the 700th Anniversary Stadium to Huay Tung Tao lake.

Chang Phueak itself is a busy residential area with a well-known night food market by the gate. It is a good place to refuel after a ride.

Getting there

Leave the Old City through Chang Phueak Gate on the northern moat, or follow Huay Kaew Road from the north-west corner.

Cycling in Huay Kaew and Chang Phueak: questions riders ask

Which bike shop in Chiang Mai is best for road bikes?

The old guide called Velocity on Huay Kaew Road the place to be for road racing, and it remains the first stop most road riders try.

Is there an easy ride from here?

Yes. The Canal Road north to Huay Tung Tao reservoir is flat and roughly 10 to 12 km each way from the university.
