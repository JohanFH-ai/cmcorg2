Hang Dong, south of Chiang Mai

Bike shops in Hang Dong

Bicycle Addict sits in Nong Khwai, on the northern edge of the district and close to the Canal Road. iBike is on Highway 108 as you head into Hang Dong town.

For riders doing the Samoeng loop, these are the last two shops before the mountains on the southern side.

Local tips

  • Stock up on tubes here before riding Road 1269. There is nothing until Samoeng.
  • Highway 108 is busy with trucks. Use the frontage roads where you can.
