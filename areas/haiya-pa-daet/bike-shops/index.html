Haiya & Pa Daet (south Chiang Mai)

Bike shops in Haiya and Pa Daet

South Chiang Mai has no single bike street, so it pays to know which shop is nearest. LA Bike Shop on Mahidol Road is closest to the Old City. Tiny Wheels hides in the lanes off Kluai Muk. Bike Corner is on Chang Khlan Road, south of the Night Bazaar, and Tommo is out near the ring road.

Local tips

  • Mahidol Road is a major artery. Use the parallel sois when riding to LA Bike Shop.
  • Call Tiny Wheels before visiting; small shops keep irregular hours.
