Cycling in Nimman and Suthep

West of the Old City, between Nimmanhaemin Road and the slopes of Doi Suthep. This is where the city's road riders gather and where the most famous climb in northern Thailand begins.

  • 3 bike shops
  • 1 classic ride
  • Mueang Chiang Mai

Riding in Nimman and Suthep

Nimman is Chiang Mai's café district, and it happens to sit right at the bottom of Doi Suthep. Riders meet here before dawn, climb to the temple and are back for coffee by eight.

Soi 13 off Nimmanhaemin Road has two bike shops a few doors apart. Brown Bike, which builds bamboo-framed bicycles, is a short ride south near Wat Suan Dok.

Away from Nimman itself, the lanes of Suthep sub-district wind through quieter neighbourhoods and university housing at the foot of the mountain.

Getting there

From Suan Dok Gate on the western moat, ride straight out along Suthep Road or take Huay Kaew Road from the north-west corner. Both take about 10 minutes.

Cycling in Nimman and Suthep: questions riders ask

Where does the Doi Suthep climb start?

At Chiang Mai Zoo, at the top of Huay Kaew Road. From Nimman it is a five-minute ride to the start.

Can I get my bike serviced in Nimman?

Yes. Jacky Bike and Best Bicycle Shop are both on Nimmanhaemin Soi 13. Velocity, the road-racing shop, is just north on Huay Kaew Road.
