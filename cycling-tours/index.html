How to choose a cycling tour

Leisure or sport?

Most tours in Chiang Mai are leisure rides on quiet farm roads east and south of the city, with stops at temples, markets and village workshops. If you want to climb mountains on a road bike, look for a specialist such as Swissbike instead.

Riding with children

Click and Travel has run tours for guests aged seven to seventy-seven and has bicycle trailers, trailer bikes and children's mountain bikes. Ask any operator what sizes they have before you book.

Check the licence

Tour operators in Thailand need a licence from the Tourism Authority of Thailand. Ask for the number and check it is printed on the website or booking confirmation.

Mountain biking

For technical singletrack and downhill trails, always ride with an experienced local guide and proper equipment.
