Hard ride

The Samoeng loop by bicycle

A paved loop of roughly 70 km around the back of Doi Suthep-Pui National Park, joining Mae Rim in the north to Hang Dong in the south via the mountain town of Samoeng. It has some vicious climbing.

The route

Leave Chiang Mai north towards Mae Rim and turn west onto Road 1096 through the Mae Sa valley. The road climbs past waterfalls and the Queen Sirikit Botanic Garden, then over the ridge to Samoeng. From Samoeng, Road 1269 takes you back over the mountains and down to Hang Dong, where Highway 108 brings you north into the city.

Riding from the Old City and back adds about 30 km of flat, busier road to the loop itself.

One day or two

A very fit rider needs seven or eight hours for the full loop. Everyone else should think about breaking it in two and sleeping in Samoeng, which has restaurants and simple accommodation. You then get both big climbs on fresh legs.

Before you go

There is little in the way of bike shops once you leave the city. Bicycle Addict and iBike in Hang Dong are the last real workshops on the southern side, so carry two tubes, a pump and a chain tool.

Descents on the 1269 are steep and twisty. Rim brakes get hot; take breaks on the way down.

At a glance

DistanceAbout 70 km for the loop, close to 100 km from the Old City and back
ClimbingSeveral long climbs, some very steep
SurfacePaved two-lane road
DifficultyHard
Time7 to 8 hours for a very fit rider, or split it over two days
Best timeNovember to February, starting at first light
